Research: Prompt Injection Reframed as Role Confusion Between Privileged and Attacker-Controlled Text

MIT researchers Charles Ye, Jasmine Cui, and Dylan Hadfield-Menell propose that prompt injection attacks are best understood as role confusion—models failing to distinguish their own privileged system-level text from attacker-controlled input wrapped in similar formats. The paper suggests that enforcing clearer role boundaries in model architecture and prompting strategies could reduce injection vulnerability.

Foundational security research with a novel theoretical framework for prompt injection has direct implications for safe agentic system deployment.
